# Round 1 Decision

## Feedback summary
- The industry and use case pitch (independent premium hair salon, Berlin, three proposed use cases) held up and should move forward.
- Use Case 2 (No-Show & Cancellation Recovery) was flagged as too complex to build as a working MVP within the Round 2 timeframe.
- Use Cases 1 (AI Booking & Inquiry Assistant) and 3 (Retention & Re-Engagement) were confirmed as appropriately scoped for the Round 2 MVP.

## Decision
KEEP

## Why
The industry, company profile, and all three proposed use cases held up in the pitch. No change to the sector, company size, or client scenario. The only adjustment is which use case(s) get built into a working MVP for Round 2.

## If CHANGE: what changes
Not applicable, decision is KEEP.

## Round 2 focus
- POC improvements: extend the existing Use Case 3 POC (already built and live-tested, `n8n/workflow.json`), and build a first working POC for Use Case 1 (booking assistant).
- MVP scope (the one capability that must run): Use Case 1 and Use Case 3. Use Case 2 is descoped for this project.
- Compliance / ROI / strategy priorities: to be scoped at Round 2 kickoff.
